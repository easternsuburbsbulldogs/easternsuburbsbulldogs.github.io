About · Our story

Our Story & History

From a meeting in Paddington in 1880 to a foundation club of the Sydney Football League. More than 140 years of Sydney football.

Sydney's oldest club

The UNSW-ES Bulldogs trace their roots to the East Sydney Football Club, formed in 1880. Today's club came together in 1999, when the University of New South Wales club merged with Eastern Suburbs.

Our story through the years

1880

East Sydney is born

A group of local enthusiasts met in Cascade Street, Paddington, and the East Sydney Football Club was formed. Its first match was played at Moore Park against Sydney, with East Sydney in dark blue and a gold sash.

1903

A founding club of the Sydney league

East Sydney was one of the founding clubs when the Sydney league began in 1903, and won its first flag that year. Jim Phelan, honoured today by the Phelan Medal, helped set the league up.

1926

East Sydney and Paddington merge

The merged club played as Eastern Suburbs until 1968, when it went back to the East Sydney name.

1953-59

Seven flags in a row

Seven back-to-back premierships, a record for the most successive flags in any senior state football league.

1962

The UNSW club is formed

The UNSW Australian National Football Club formed on 22 March 1962. It played in its own right from 1965, won its first premiership in 1966, and earned promotion to first grade in 1969.

1980-84

Five straight premierships

The 1980 grand final score of 30.24 (204) against North Shore still stands as a league record for a premiership decider.

1999

Two clubs become the UNSW-ES Bulldogs

The University of New South Wales club merged with Eastern Suburbs, so students and locals in Sydney's east now play for the same club.

2018-23

Four flags in six seasons

Five successive Men's Premier Division grand finals for four premierships: back-to-back in 2018-19 and again in 2022-23.

2027

The SFL era

The Bulldogs are one of nine foundation clubs in the Sydney Football League, NSW's new premier competition. Round 1 is Saturday 3 April 2027.
