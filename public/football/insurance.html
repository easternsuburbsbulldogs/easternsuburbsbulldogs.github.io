Football · Insurance

Player Insurance

What you're covered for, why private health insurance matters, and how to claim.

Please read before you play. Every registered Bulldog has base-level personal accident cover. It is not comprehensive, so the club strongly recommends you also hold your own private health insurance.

What you're covered for

Provider

Chubb, through UNSW Sport

Members and volunteers of UNSW sports clubs, including the Bulldogs, have personal accident cover with Chubb Insurance, arranged through UNSW Sport and Recreation (Arc).

Level of cover

Base-level only

It does not substantially cover private hospital treatment. Some non-Medicare expenses are partly covered, but an excess and a percentage of costs are not.

Why private health insurance matters

If you rely only on the UNSW accident cover, you may be treated through Medicare in the public system, where waits can be long, including for surgery. That's why the club strongly recommends holding your own private health insurance as well.

Who can claim

Eligibility

Fees fully paid

Your UNSW-ES membership fees must be paid in full.

Eligibility

Injured with the club

The injury must happen while training or playing for the UNSW-ES Bulldogs.

How to claim

Claims are made through Arc. The claim form and FAQs are on the Arc Sport insurance page.
