From our colours to 289 games: Dane Rampe retires

Before the 289 games, the Bob Skilton Medal and the All-Australian blazer, Dane Rampe was a Bulldog.

Dane Rampe has called time on a 14-season AFL career with the Sydney Swans. His 289 games in the red and white are the seventh-most in Swans history.

289AFL games
2016All-Australian
2019Bob Skilton Medal

The Bulldogs chapter

Rampe's path to the AFL wasn't the usual one. Overlooked as a teenager, he spent three seasons in the VFL with Williamstown before coming home to Sydney and joining the UNSW-ES Bulldogs.

In 2012 he dominated the local competition. He won the Phelan Medal as AFL Sydney's best and fairest, and helped the Bulldogs to the premiership. The Swans took him with pick 37 in that year's rookie draft, and he debuted in Round 1, 2013.

A career at the top

Rampe was named in the All-Australian team in 2016, won the Bob Skilton Medal as the Swans' best and fairest in 2019, and served as co-captain from 2019 to 2023. In his final season he led the side again through the 2026 finals series.

Congratulations, Dane, from everyone at the kennel. Once a Bulldog, always a Bulldog.
