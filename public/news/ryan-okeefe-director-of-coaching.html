Ryan O'Keefe joins as Director of Coaching

A two-time premiership Swan is coming to the kennel. Ryan O'Keefe will oversee coaching across our Sydney Football League teams in 2027.

Ryan O'Keefe has joined the UNSW-ES Bulldogs as Director of Coaching for the club's Sydney Football League program. He will work with our SFL coaches and playing groups as we prepare for the competition's first season.

2012Norm Smith Medal

The playing career

O'Keefe played 286 games for the Swans before retiring in 2014. He won premierships in 2005 and 2012, and took the Norm Smith Medal as best on ground in the 2012 Grand Final. He also won the Bob Skilton Medal as the Swans' best and fairest in 2009 and was named All-Australian in 2006.

Since football

After playing, O'Keefe moved into coaching and football development, and more recently into high-performance coaching and leadership consulting.

His role at the Bulldogs

He will focus on our SFL teams, working alongside head coaches Jordan Doering and Tom Williamson and their staff. Pre-season is on now, Monday nights at Robertson Road Fields, Centennial Park.

Welcome to the kennel, Ryan.
