Bulldogs named a Sydney Football League foundation club

From 2027 the Bulldogs will play in the Sydney Football League, the new premier competition for Australian football in New South Wales.

The AFL has confirmed the Sydney Football League (SFL) as the state's premier competition from 2027, and the UNSW-ES Bulldogs are one of nine clubs awarded a foundation licence. The licences followed a competitive tender and assessment process, with the Bulldogs representing the East-Bayside region.

Players from all nine clubs gathered at Henson Park on 12 August for the official announcement, alongside AFL Chief Operating Officer Tom Harley and Head of AFL NSW/ACT Andrew Varasdi.

What the SFL looks like

The competition starts in April 2027 across four divisions: Senior Men's, Senior Women's, Under 19.5 Men's and Under 20 Women's. Each round, the SFL's Match of the Round in the Senior Men's and Senior Women's will stream free on Kayo Freebies.

What it means for the Bulldogs

Nineteen Bulldogs have already made AFL and AFLW lists. In the SFL our players will face the strongest senior football in NSW every week. Whether you're a returning player, a student, or new to the game, there's a place for you in 2027.
